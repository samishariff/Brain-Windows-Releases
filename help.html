A little guidance

Get help with Brain.

The next Windows preview is being prepared. Download and setup instructions will appear here when it is ready to try.

If you already have a preview

If Sami has already shared a copy with you, keep that copy and any meeting backups. Ask Sami for help before removing the app or replacing files. No new download or automatic update is available from this website yet.

Ask Sami for help

Use your usual private way of contacting Sami. Describe what you were trying to do and what appeared on the screen. If you share a screenshot, check that it does not show meeting text, account information, or private calendar links.

Keep recordings, private notes, passwords, and sign-in codes out of public messages.

Before trying a recording

Tell everyone before you record. Start with a short practice conversation and listen to it afterward. Keep another copy of anything important while you are trying an early preview.
