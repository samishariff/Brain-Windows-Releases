Your privacy

Your meetings are personal.

Brain is shared by Sami Shariff. This website is being prepared for the next Windows preview; downloads are not available yet.

On your PC

Brain stores recordings, transcripts, notes, and meeting records in your own Windows account. Its on-device speech and summary tools process meeting content on your PC. Brain does not automatically send your meetings to Sami.

The speech and summary tools need an initial download. Download providers receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address.

Services you choose

If you choose an online AI service, your requests can include meeting text and chat questions. That provider's privacy settings apply. Choose on-device tools if you want that processing to stay on your PC.

Calendar files and private calendar links can include event titles, attendees, and other personal details. Share them carefully. Google and Microsoft calendar sign-in is still being prepared for the Windows preview.

Backups and getting help

Meeting backups can contain private conversations. Keep them somewhere you trust. Deleting a meeting from Brain does not erase older backup copies.

Contact Sami privately for help. Review screenshots or reports before sharing them, and keep recordings, meeting text, account details, and private calendar links out of public messages.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It does not ask you to sign in or upload meeting content. These pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies added by Brain. GitHub receives ordinary connection information when you visit.
